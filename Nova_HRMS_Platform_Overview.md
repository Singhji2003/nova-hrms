# Nova HRMS - Platform Overview

Welcome to **Nova HRMS**, the industry-leading, next-generation Human Resources SaaS platform. Designed with a stunning Apple-like glassmorphism interface, Nova HRMS provides a comprehensive suite of tools to manage the entire employee lifecycle—from recruitment to offboarding.

---

## 🚀 Core Platform Architecture

- **Multi-Tenant Architecture**: Supports multiple companies on a single platform. Super Admins can manage companies, while HR Admins manage their specific company data.
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: Secure access tiers for Super Admins, HR Managers, and Employees (Self-Service).
- **Premium Glassmorphism UI**: Beautiful, frosted-glass aesthetic with micro-animations and perfectly responsive dashboards.

---

## 🌟 Feature Modules & Capabilities

### 1. Employee Data Management & Org Structure
- **Centralized Database**: Store personal, professional, and financial records securely.
- **Dynamic Organization Chart**: Automatically generates a visual, hierarchical org chart based on manager-reportee relationships.
- **Employee Directory**: A searchable "colleagues" directory for internal networking.

### 2. Time, Attendance & Leaves
- **Geofenced Check-in/Check-out**: Employees can log their attendance; the system enforces location-based rules and IP tracking.
- **Leave Management System**: Define custom leave types (Sick, Casual, Earned) with automated accrual and manager approval workflows.
- **Overtime Calculation**: Automatic tracking of standard hours vs. overtime.

### 3. Payroll & Compliance
- **One-Click Payroll Run**: Generate monthly payroll batches for all employees instantly.
- **Automated Deductions**: System computes basic pay, HRA, allowances, tax (TDS), and provident fund (PF) deductions.
- **Payslip Generation**: Employees can download beautifully formatted PDF payslips directly from their portal.

### 4. Performance & Goals (Appraisals)
- **OKR & Goal Tracking**: Employees can set personal goals and track their progress.
- **360-Degree Appraisals**: HR can initiate performance review cycles. Managers can rate employees, provide feedback, and track historical performance.

### 5. Talent Acquisition (AI-Powered ATS)
- **Job Postings**: Create and publish internal/external job requisitions.
- **AI Resume Match**: The ATS automatically scans candidate resumes against the job description, generating an "AI Match Score" and a summary of their fit.
- **Kanban Pipeline**: Drag-and-drop candidates through stages (Applied, Screening, Interview, Offered).

### 6. Learning Management System (LMS)
- **Course Builder**: HR can create training modules, compliance courses, and onboarding material.
- **Assignment & Tracking**: Assign courses to specific employees and monitor their completion progress.
- **Employee Learning Portal**: A dedicated space for employees to consume content and upskill.

### 7. Culture & Employee Engagement
- **Kudos Wall**: A social feed where employees can give peer-to-peer recognition, fostering a positive work environment.
- **eNPS Pulse Surveys**: HR can launch anonymous company-wide pulse surveys to measure Employee Net Promoter Score and gather feedback.
- **Company Announcements**: Broadcast critical news to the entire organization.

### 8. Document Vault & KYC
- **Secure File Storage**: HR can request KYC documents (ID, Tax forms, Contracts) from employees.
- **Approval Workflows**: Employees upload documents; HR reviews and verifies them in a centralized dashboard.

### 9. Helpdesk & IT Assets
- **Internal Ticketing System**: Employees can raise support tickets (IT, HR, Finance).
- **Asset Management**: Track company laptops, phones, and equipment assigned to employees.

### 10. Resignation & Offboarding
- **Automated Workflows**: Employees can submit resignation requests.
- **Clearance Pipeline**: HR tracks clearance tasks (IT asset return, knowledge transfer) leading to a Full & Final (F&F) settlement.

### 11. Advanced Shift Rostering
- **Spreadsheet Scheduling**: HR has a massive, high-level view to assign Morning, Evening, Night, or Off shifts to any employee.
- **My Schedule**: Employees view their exact assigned shift timings for the week.

## 🔄 Standard User Flow

### For Super Admins
1. Log into the centralized control panel.
2. Register a new Client Company (e.g., "Acme Corp").
3. Create the initial HR Admin account for Acme Corp.
4. Monitor platform-wide revenue and cross-company analytics.

### For HR Managers
1. Log in to the HR Dashboard and view company-specific analytics (Headcount, Payroll Expense, Attrition).
2. Set up Departments, Designations, and Company Policies.
3. Add Employees to the system (or import them).
4. Daily Tasks: Approve leaves, resolve support tickets, post jobs on the ATS, and review KYC documents.
5. Monthly Tasks: Run payroll, initiate performance reviews, and launch pulse surveys.

### For Employees (ESS)
1. Log in to the Employee Self-Service portal.
2. Dashboard view: Clock-in for the day, view pending tasks, and see company announcements.
3. Request time off or log an IT support ticket.
4. View assigned training courses and submit required KYC documents.
5. Download monthly payslips and give "Kudos" to a helpful teammate.
